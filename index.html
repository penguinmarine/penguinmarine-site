About us

About our reliable and efficient ship maritime services

Welcome to Penguin Marine Agency, a trusted name with over 12 years of dedicated maritime expertise. We specialize in efficient vessel assistance, handling diverse cargo types including dry, liquid, break bulk, and project cargo. Our commitment to excellence, combined with round-the-clock availability, ensures seamless operations and timely deliveries for clients worldwide.

What we do

Delivering your goods safely and on time

Penguin Marine Agency stands as your expert maritime partner. Our focus lies in seamless vessel support and managing an array of cargoes: dry, liquid, break bulk, and project. From charterers’ needs to global deliveries, experience reliability, efficiency, and global reach.
